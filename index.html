Tensio Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

Tensio (“the app”) is a blood pressure diary app for Android provided by susuim (“we”). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • Your blood pressure records are stored on your device and, if Android backup is turned on, in your own Google account backup. We do not operate servers and cannot see your records.
  • No account or sign-up is required.
  • Health data is never used for advertising and is never sold or shared with advertisers.
  • Ads (Google AdMob) collect limited device information, as described below.

Information stored on your device

Readings you enter (systolic and diastolic pressure, pulse, time, posture, arm, tags and notes), profile details you choose to enter (name, birth year, sex), reminder settings and app settings are stored in a local database on your device. This information is not sent to us.

Device backup

If Android backup is turned on in your phone settings, Android may include the app’s data (your readings and settings) in your Google account backup so it can be restored when you set up a new phone. Google manages this backup in your account, and on recent Android versions it is encrypted with your screen lock. We cannot access it. You can turn off backup in your phone’s backup settings.

Health Connect

If you turn on Health Connect sync, the app reads blood pressure and heart rate records from Health Connect to show them in your diary, and writes the readings you enter to Health Connect so that other apps you allow can use them. The app accesses only the data types you permit, and you can withdraw permission at any time in Health Connect settings. Data from Health Connect stays on your device and is not sent to us or to third parties.

Tensio’s use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Exports

You can export your records as a PDF report or CSV file and share them through apps you choose, such as email or messaging apps. Those apps handle the file under their own policies.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect device identifiers such as the advertising ID, IP address and approximate location derived from it, and ad interaction data. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in the app settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Your health data is never shared with AdMob or used to select ads. Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Permissions

  • Notifications: to send the measurement reminders you set.
  • Alarms and reminders: to deliver reminders at the exact time you choose.
  • Health Connect: to read and write blood pressure and heart rate, only if you turn on sync.
  • Internet: to show ads. Your health records are not sent to us or to advertisers; they leave your phone only if you share them yourself or through Android backup to your own Google account.

Retention and deletion

Your records remain on your device until you delete them. Settings → Delete all data removes everything the app stores, and uninstalling the app also removes it. Data written to Health Connect can be deleted in Health Connect. Advertising data is kept by Google for a limited period under Google’s retention settings.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, California law, Brazil’s LGPD or Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. Because your health records stay on your device, you can view, edit, export and delete them directly in the app. For advertising data, contact us at the address below or use Google’s privacy tools. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

Medical disclaimer

Tensio is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. Always consult a healthcare professional about your blood pressure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
